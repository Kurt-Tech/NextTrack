## Invitation

You are invited to take part in a pilot evaluation of NextTrack, a music
recommendation prototype developed as part of a Computer Science final
project.

Before deciding whether to participate, please read the information
below. It explains why the pilot is being conducted, what you will be
asked to do, and how your responses will be used.

Participation is voluntary.

---

## What is NextTrack?

NextTrack is a prototype music recommendation system designed to explore
how different levels of recommendation diversity and exploration can be
balanced with contextual relevance.

The system generates recommendations using a recent listening context
consisting of three tracks.

The prototype also includes controls for exploring different
recommendation behaviours. However, during the controlled study you will
compare recommendation sets without being shown the internal settings
used to generate them.

---

## What is the purpose of this pilot?

The pilot has two purposes:

1. to test whether the NextTrack evaluation interface and recommendation
   comparison procedure work as intended; and
2. to identify unclear instructions, technical problems or questionnaire
   issues before the formal user evaluation is conducted.

Pilot responses will be reviewed separately from the formal study
responses.

---

## What will I be asked to do?

If you agree to participate, you will:

1. receive a participant code;
2. use the NextTrack Study Mode interface;
3. evaluate three listening contexts:
   - Rock;
   - Hip-Hop; and
   - Country;
4. review three recent listening tracks for each context;
5. generate two recommendation sets labelled Set A and Set B;
6. compare the two recommendation sets;
7. optionally open tracks in Spotify if you would like to listen to
   unfamiliar recommendations;
8. rate the recommendation sets for:
   - relevance;
   - diversity;
   - usefulness for music discovery; and
   - overall satisfaction;
9. indicate which recommendation set you prefer; and
10. provide feedback about the interface, instructions and pilot
    procedure.

The settings used to generate Recommendation Set A and Recommendation
Set B will not be shown during the evaluation. This is intended to avoid
influencing your judgement.

---

## How long will participation take?

The pilot is expected to take approximately 10–15 minutes.

The actual completion time is one of the aspects being assessed during
the pilot, so it may take slightly more or less time.

You are not expected to listen to every recommended track in full.
Spotify links are provided so that you can briefly listen to unfamiliar
tracks where this is helpful to your judgement.

---

## Do I have to participate?

No.

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate,
and you may stop the evaluation at any time before submitting your
responses without giving a reason.

Choosing not to participate will not result in any disadvantage or
penalty.

## What information will be collected?

The pilot questionnaire will collect:

- a participant code;
- general information about music streaming and recommendation usage;
- ratings of Recommendation Set A and Recommendation Set B;
- recommendation-set preferences;
- optional written comments;
- feedback about the evaluation interface and instructions;
- reports of any technical problems; and
- approximate completion time.

The study does not require your name.

## How will my responses be used?

Responses will be used to:

- assess whether the pilot procedure works as intended;
- identify improvements required before the formal evaluation;
- support evaluation of the NextTrack final-year project; and
- potentially contribute to aggregated findings discussed in the final
  project report.

Pilot findings should be reported in summary form rather than by
identifying individual participants.

Direct comments may be quoted in the report where useful, but they
should not be associated with your name.

## Are there any risks?

This is considered a low-risk evaluation.

Possible inconveniences include:

- the time required to complete the evaluation;
- unfamiliarity with some recommended music;
- minor frustration if a Spotify link or prototype feature does not work
  as expected; and
- fatigue from comparing multiple recommendation sets.

You may stop participating at any time.

---

## Are there any benefits?

There is no guaranteed personal benefit from participating.

Your feedback will help evaluate and improve the NextTrack prototype and
the design of the formal user study.

---

## Use of Spotify

NextTrack provides links to Spotify so that participants can listen to
tracks if required.

Using these links is optional.

Spotify is an external service and is not part of the NextTrack research
system. If you choose to open Spotify, your use of Spotify is subject to
Spotify's own terms, privacy practices and account settings.

A Spotify account is not required to provide questionnaire ratings based
on the information shown in NextTrack.

---

## Confidentiality

Your responses will be treated confidentially within the limits of the
study.

Results presented in the project report will be aggregated or
pseudonymised, and the report will not intentionally identify individual
